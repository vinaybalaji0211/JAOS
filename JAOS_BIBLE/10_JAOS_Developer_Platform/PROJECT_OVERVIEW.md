\# What is JAOS?



JAOS (Jarvis Artificial Operating System) is an intelligent operating system designed to understand user intent, reason about tasks, manage knowledge, and execute actions across computers, mobile devices, cloud services, and future robotic platforms.



JAOS is not a chatbot.



JAOS is an AI Operating System.



\---



\# Vision



Build one intelligent system capable of assisting users naturally while remaining modular, secure, transparent, and scalable.



Long-term goals include:



\- Personal AI Assistant

\- Development Assistant

\- Research Assistant

\- Automation Platform

\- Multi-device Intelligence

\- Robotics Integration
