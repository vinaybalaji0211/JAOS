\# Lesson 2 — JAOS Architecture



The architecture separates responsibilities into layers.



User



↓



Executive Brain



↓



Kernel



↓



Platform Layer



↓



External Systems



\---



Executive Brain



Responsible for reasoning, planning, memory coordination, and decision making.



Kernel



Responsible for execution.



Platform Layer



Responsible for interacting with operating systems, applications, devices, APIs, and services.



This separation keeps JAOS maintainable and scalable.
