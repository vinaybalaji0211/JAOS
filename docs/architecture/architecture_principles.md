\# JAOS Architecture Principles



\## Principle 1



Models own data.



Engines own behavior.



\---



\## Principle 2



High cohesion.



Low coupling.



\---



\## Principle 3



Separate thinking from execution.



Executive Brain thinks.



Kernel executes.



\---



\## Principle 4



Avoid premature abstraction.



Duplicate first.



Abstract later.



\---



\## Principle 5



Every component has one responsibility.



\---



\## Principle 6



Every architecture change must be reviewed before implementation.



\---



\## Principle 7



Alpha focuses on correctness.



Beta focuses on capability.



Gamma focuses on optimization.
