\# JEH-002 — JAOS Mission



Version: 1.0.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Vinay



\---



\## Mission



Design and build an AI Operating System that assists humans by understanding intent, planning intelligently, executing safely, and continuously evolving through a modular architecture.



JAOS should become a trusted digital companion that combines reasoning, automation, memory, and explainability while always respecting user control.



\---



\## Core Objectives



\- Build a modular architecture.

\- Support long-term scalability.

\- Prioritize safety and transparency.

\- Enable future collaboration.

\- Preserve engineering knowledge.

\- Deliver maintainable software.
