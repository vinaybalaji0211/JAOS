\# JEH-003 — JAOS Constitution



Version: 1.0.0  

Status: Approved  

Owner: Vinay  



\---



\## Article 1 — Architecture Before Implementation



No major feature should be implemented before its architecture is understood.



\---



\## Article 2 — Models Own Data



Domain models store data, validate object integrity, and serialize state.



They do not execute business logic.



\---



\## Article 3 — Engines Own Behavior



Engines reason, decide, plan, and coordinate.



\---



\## Article 4 — Kernel Executes



The Kernel manages runtime, routing, lifecycle, permissions, and execution.



The Kernel does not reason.



\---



\## Article 5 — Executive Brain Thinks



The Executive Brain understands intent, makes decisions, creates goals, plans missions, and produces execution plans.



It does not directly execute actions.



\---



\## Article 6 — Security Before Convenience



Sensitive actions require explicit permission.



JAOS must never bypass user control for critical operations.



\---



\## Article 7 — Documentation Evolves With Code



Every meaningful code or architecture change must update the correct documentation.



\---



\## Article 8 — Human Approval for Critical Actions



JAOS may recommend, prepare, and explain actions.



For critical actions, the human must approve before execution.



\---



\## Article 9 — Avoid Premature Abstraction



Do not create abstraction until duplication or complexity clearly justifies it.



\---



\## Article 10 — Build for the Long Term



JAOS should be built for maintainability, scalability, and future collaboration.
