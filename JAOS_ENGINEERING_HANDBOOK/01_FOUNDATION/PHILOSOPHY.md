\## Engineering Philosophy



Architecture before implementation.



Documentation grows with the project.



Knowledge belongs to the project.



Simple solutions are preferred over unnecessary complexity.



Security is never sacrificed for convenience.



Every feature must have a purpose.



Every subsystem should have one responsibility.



Every engineering decision should improve long-term maintainability.



Build today with future contributors in mind.
