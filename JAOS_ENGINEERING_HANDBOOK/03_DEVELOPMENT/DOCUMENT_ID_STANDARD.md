\# JEH-DEV-006 — Document ID Standard



Document IDs uniquely identify every engineering document.



Examples



JEH-001



JEH-ARCH-001



JEH-DEV-001



JEH-COL-001



JEH-OPS-001



JEH-KB-001



JEH-FUT-001



Rules



\- IDs never change.

\- IDs are never reused.

\- Deprecated documents keep their IDs.
